Only from officially sourced numbers, and always with n

A correlation without an n is a rumour. Every value on this page is computed from the official game log or the official standings, carries its sample size, and carries a 95% interval. Where the required input does not exist, the cell is blank and the reason is printed.

The estimate moves — say so

HOW TO READ IT

The number a same-game parlay depends on

The margin-to-total correlation decides whether a big favourite and an over are really independent legs. In the full-season window the estimate is negative but its 95% interval includes zero, so it cannot be stated as a finding. In the weeks 6–9 window the same calculation is more strongly negative and its interval excludes zero — but that is 54 games, and the two windows disagreeing is exactly why a single-season correlation should be treated as a hypothesis with a wide interval rather than as a coefficient to price with. A parlay priced as if these legs were independent would be overstated; a parlay priced with either point estimate would be overconfident. That is the honest conclusion.
