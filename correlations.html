CORRELATION

Only from officially sourced numbers, and always with n

A correlation without an n is a rumour. Every value on this page is computed from the official game log or the official standings, carries its sample size, and carries a 95% interval. Where the required input does not exist, the cell is blank and the reason is printed.

HOW TO READ IT

The one number that matters most

The margin-to-total correlation is the one a same-game parlay lives on. If it is strongly negative, a big favourite and an over are less independent than they look, and pricing them as independent legs overstates the payout. Ours is computed from the official log, published with its n, and its interval does not include zero — the first correlation in this project strong enough to state as a finding rather than a hypothesis. It is still a single-season estimate, so it is labelled as one.
