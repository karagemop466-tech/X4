Line Origination & Market

How to originate our own NFL price rather than copy the market's — and, first, an honest account of what a price even is and where it can come from.

The blocking finding. There is no official league source for betting prices. The NFL publishes standings, schedules, injury reports and roster rules; it does not publish odds. Prices are commercial products of licensed operators. This means the project's "official source only" rule cannot be satisfied for any odds-derived number — which gates closing-line value, every correlation estimate that needs prices, and the entire edge calculation. The two pieces of arithmetic that matter most

1. De-vig before comparing

A published price contains the book's margin. Comparing a fair model price against a raw market price manufactures an edge out of nothing. Both sides must be de-vigged with the same convention before they are compared. The convention has not been ratified yet (Q-24) — and until it is, no comparison is trustworthy, because different de-vig methods give different answers.

2. Closing-line value per bet

For each bet, CLV is the price at the moment of the bet divided by the fair closing price, both de-vigged. Test the mean of that ratio across the sample against 1.00. Two practical cautions from the cited research:

  • The dispersion of the ratio grows with the odds — reported standard deviation of about 0.043 at odds of 1.25 versus about 0.14 at odds of 5.0 — so the threshold must be odds-dependent, not a single fixed number.
  • Practitioner guidance suggests a bettor must beat the fair closing price roughly 70% of the time to have any positive expected value at all. That is a high bar, and it is the right one.

What originating a line is not

  • Not averaging other books. That is a consensus price, not an originated one. It contains no independent information.
  • Not a "model" that is fitted until it matches the market. A model fitted to the market can only reproduce the market; by construction it will never beat the closing line, which is the only thing we measure skill by.
  • Not a prediction of the winner. The output is a fair probability with an uncertainty, which is only useful once compared to a de-vigged market price.

What is actually available to build with, right now

Official, verified and already loaded: standings for all 32 clubs, the full Week 10 schedule with the official bye list, 345 official injury-report rows, the official injury-report designation definitions, and the league's roster and elevation rules. That is enough to build the situational and personnel layers of the model. It is not enough to produce a price that can be scored, because scoring needs the closing line.
