What this page cannot tell you

Whether any strategy makes money. The corpus supports a backtest, and one has been run, but the sample is small, the pre-registered minimum is not met, and the multiplicity adjustment is not yet computed. Until those clear, a positive return here would be indistinguishable from luck, and the site's rule is that such a result is reported as ineligible rather than published as a win. The three inputs that would change the answer are: a pre-kickoff price captured for every originated market, a larger settled sample, and inactive lists to replace the injury designation with the fact of who actually played.
