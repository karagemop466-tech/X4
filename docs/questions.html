Open Questions

The questions that need answering before any decision can reasonably be made — split into what we already have (verified) and what we do not (unverified), plus a concrete method for discovering each missing piece.

16 of the 32 questions are P0. Filter by Priority = P0 to see them. The project can be documented and designed without them, but it cannot safely be operated without them. Chief among them: whether real money is involved at all (Q-01, Q-02), and where the data will come from (Q-09, Q-10, Q-11). Several P0 items only bite if the mode is set to real money — bankroll, stop limits, operator selection and the promotion owner are P1 precisely because the recommended default is paper trading.

What "we do not have" looks like

The What we do NOT have column is the honest half of this page. It is not a to-do list of small tasks; most of the gaps are structural:

  • No odds data of any kind. Not opening prices, not closing prices, not prop prices. Without closing prices, closing-line value — the core anti-luck measurement — cannot be computed at all. Blocked on Q-11.
  • No play-by-play or participation data. Every offensive and defensive model needs it. Blocked on Q-09.
  • No depth/string labels. No official source publishes them. Blocked on Q-17.
  • No bankroll, no limits, no staking rule. These are user decisions, not derivable from data. Blocked on Q-05 through Q-07.
  • No jurisdiction. Which determines legality, tax and whether any of the above is even permitted. Blocked on Q-01.

Note what is not in that list: standings, schedule, byes, injuries and roster rules. Those are settled, verified and published elsewhere on this site.

Suggestions for discovering the missing information

The How to discover it column on each row is the short answer. The long answers are suggestions S-08 through S-18, which cover data licensing, the bet-log schema, the correlation estimation protocol, the depth pipeline, operator capacity and the leaderboard's multiplicity correction.

Every "What we already know" cell links to a primary source in the Source Register. Every "What we do NOT have" cell is blank or explicitly marked unverified.
