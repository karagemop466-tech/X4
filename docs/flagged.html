The two structural flags, in plain terms

F-02 · Prices
No official source
The league does not publish odds. Without a licensed price feed there is no closing line, without a closing line there is no CLV, and without CLV the project's main anti-luck instrument does not work. This is the highest-leverage unresolved item.
F-03 · Depth
No official source
NFL.com 404s on depth charts; club sites publish ordered lists without string labels. Requirements R-16 and R-17 cannot be met from an official source — only derived, and derived values must be labelled as derived.

What was NOT flagged, and why

Restraint matters as much as flagging. The following looked odd but were not flagged, because flagging them would have been an error:

  • Player–team assignments that differ from prior seasons. Aaron Donald appearing on the Rams' injury report looks wrong to anyone carrying 2024 knowledge — but NFL.com reports he unretired to play for the Rams in 2026. Similarly for other roster changes. Rule: never flag a roster entry on the basis of prior-season knowledge (F-09).
  • Teams on bye in Week 10 (Chicago, Denver, Philadelphia, Tampa Bay). These are listed explicitly on the official schedule page under "Teams on Bye", so they are verified, not inferred.
  • Clubs having played different numbers of games. That is normal at this point in a season. It is flagged only as an interpretation caution (F-14) about comparing raw points-for and points-against across clubs — not as a data error.

How to clear a flag

  1. Read the Source link on the row.
  2. Adjudicate against a primary source — the league, the club, the regulator, or the publisher's own documentation.
  3. Update the affected data file in docs/data/ and set the row's status to verified.
  4. Record the resolution and its date in COORDINATION.md.
  5. Where two sources genuinely disagree, keep both links and leave the value blank. Do not average them and do not pick the convenient one.
