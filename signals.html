Why this removes luck: luck loves untested multiplicity (try 100 strategies, keep the winner), unguarded samples (fit and evaluate on the same rows) and short streaks (10 lucky bets). G2+G3+G6 attack exactly those. What remains after all 8 gates is selectively skill-based — not guaranteed, just no longer explainable by the usual lucky processes.
SIGNAL vs STAKING

Two questions, never one number

A strategy can be right about the game and still be a bad bet, and it can be a good bet while being right for the wrong reason. So the leaderboard publishes two separate things: signal (is the prediction better than the baseline?) and staking (what a bankroll would have done). They are never collapsed into one score.

Why the staking column cannot be filled yet. Staking needs a price at the moment the bet is taken. This project has never taken a price at stake time — the store holds open, current and close, not stake — so every CLV cell is published blank and labelled blocked rather than filled with the model-to-close disagreement, which is a different quantity (Flag F-20). The net-units column is therefore a paper-trading record, not a return.
What the numbers here are. Nine graded rows from the settled Week 10 game, one bet per model. The hit rates are 0.00 or 1.00 because n = 1 per model: they are a wiring check, not a skill estimate. Nothing on this page scores a strategy as profitable. The simulation that shows why the two columns must stay separate — a 2.4-point true edge turning into +78.18 flat and −83.21 at 5% of bankroll from the same 100-unit start — is on Integrity & programme.

Parlay implication (the one that matters today)

Margin ↔ total is negative (r = −0.32, CI excludes 0): in high-scoring games the margin tends to shrink. A "favorite covers + over" parlay therefore has legs that move against each other — the true joint hit rate is below the naive product of the singles, so an independence-priced payout is worse than it looks. This is why gate G7 exists. Opponent scores show no usable correlation in this window (r ≈ 0.02), so "both teams over" legs cannot assume dependence either way yet.
