What “Questionable” actually meant in Week 10

Every player on the official Week 10 injury report who carried a game status, joined to the official Game Book for his own game and scored on whether he actually played. The interval is a 95% Wilson score interval; n is printed because a rate without n is not a measurement. This is one week of one season — it is a first estimate, not a constant, and it is not used as a model feature until the sample is large enough for the interval to be useful.
