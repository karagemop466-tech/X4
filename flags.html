F-17The three published layers define three different validation gatesIrregularityresolvedThe Strategy Lab on this site scores seven gates (G1 data integrity, G2 out-of-sample, G3 minimum sample, G4 significance, G5 stability, G6 calibration, G7 not the benchmark). No two layers use the same inventory.The main site's seven are the gates that can actually be scored from the official data that exists; the other two are written as designs for gates that cannot yet be evaluated. F-18Market betting lines are not an official NFL publicationUnverified dataresolvedThe league publishes no betting lines. This site does the second: the price is never presented as an NFL line, the source is printed in the row, and the difference column (fair minus market) is published as its own column so a disagreement is never read as an edge. Where the feed had no price, the cell stays blank and says so.Official source ↗ Evidence ↗
