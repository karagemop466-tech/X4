Injuries and depth deficiencies

The official NFL.com Week 10 injury report, transcribed in full: every listed player, their position, the injury, the practice designation and the game status. NFL clubs publish these under the league's injury-report policy, which is why they are the authoritative record.

Source: nfl.com/injuries
The public report is not the whole story. Players on injured reserve, the reserve/nontheatre injured list or the physically-unable-to-perform list do not appear in the public weekly report. Those absences show up in league news instead - see the next two tables. This is recorded as flag F-03.
