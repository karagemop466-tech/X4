Source: the official NFL.com Week 10 injury report, <https://www.nfl.com/injuries/>, retrieved 14 November 2026, for the 28 teams playing in Week 10.

## What the official report contains

For each listed player: the listed position, the injury description, the practice participation code and the game status. The practice codes are Full Participation in Practice, Limited Participation in Practice, and Did Not Participate In Practice; the game statuses are Out, Doubtful and Questionable. A blank status means the player was listed with no designation.

## What the report cannot tell us

Two gaps, both material to any depth analysis:

**1. Players on injured reserve do not appear.** The public weekly report covers players expected to take part in the upcoming game. Baltimore's starting quarterback appears nowhere in the Week 10 table - his absence surfaces only in the league's own news reporting, where the Ravens confirm he is expected to miss six to ten weeks. Any availability model built from the public report alone will silently miss exactly the absences that move a line the most. This is flag F-03.

**2. There is no depth-chart string.** The report says who is available; it does not say who the second, third or fourth string is at a position. The NFL publishes no consolidated league depth chart, so no player on this site is assigned a depth rank. This is flag F-02.

## Unit depth deficiencies that can be evidenced

A depth deficiency means a position group has lost its starter and the replacement level is unknown or lower. The following can be stated from the official report and the league's own reporting without inferring anything:

**Green Bay - running back.** Josh Jacobs is Out with a finger injury and the league reports he is to undergo surgery, with the return timeline to be determined. A genuine absence of the team's leading back rather than a doubt.

**Minnesota - quarterback and tight end.** Kyler Murray and T.J. Hockenson are both Out, so the replacement at quarterback is a backup by definition. The league also confirmed J.J. McCarthy was expected to play in Week 10.

**San Francisco - wide receiver.** Mike Evans is Out with an Achilles injury. With Deebo Samuel and Demarcus Robinson listed and no status, the depth behind the top receiver is unclear.

**Seattle - quarterback.** Sam Darnold is Out with ribs and quadricep injuries, the league confirmed he is ruled out for the Week 10 game against the Raiders, and Drew Lock is expected to start. A confirmed backup starting is a measurable event, unlike an inferred depth rank.

**Cleveland and Arizona - quarterback.** The league confirmed Deshaun Watson starts for Cleveland against Houston and Carson Beck starts for Arizona against the LA Rams.

**Miami - quarterback.** Malik Willis was placed on injured reserve with a groin injury, with Kyle McCord in line for a first start.

**Baltimore - quarterback.** Lamar Jackson is out with a thumb injury; the league gives different windows in two separate items - six to nine weeks in one, six to ten in another. That disagreement is recorded as flag F-04 and is not resolved here.

**LA Rams - wide receiver.** Puka Nacua is likely to be placed on injured reserve with turf toe and sprained knee ligaments.

## Position-group definition

The Offense / Defense / Special teams split and the position-group names used in the roll-up are this project's grouping of each player's **listed** position on the official report. They are a reporting convenience, not an official classification, and they say nothing about a player's actual role or snap share.
