# Independent repository and source-policy audit — 2026-11-14

## Executive finding

The primary NFL Decision Lab and its build checks are reproducible and the published facts in the primary site are source-linked. This audit found one material exception to the brief's strict official-source-only rule: the separate decision-desk pages publish a 13-game DraftKings spread/total snapshot, attributed to ESPN (source tier T3). These odds are not published or confirmed by the NFL. They are now explicitly flagged as **unverified under the official-only policy** in the desk's Flagged for Review page (R-14) and the README. Do not treat them as official values or eligible evidence for a strategy decision.

No strategy is currently reward-eligible. The model/validation pipeline reproduces nine strategy evaluations over 105 walk-forward games, but none passes every gate; market prices, player-prop data and official depth-chart ranks remain absent or unverified.

## Findings and irregularities

1. **T3 market snapshot violates an official-only interpretation.** ESPN is identified in the source register as a trusted publisher, not an official source. It supplies 13 DraftKings odds rows in the desk data, and those values flow to `data.html`, `signals.html`, and the computed stats JSON. The main Decision Lab's model-line page correctly leaves market lines blank. R-14 now calls out the exception. Until the project formally authorizes non-NFL odds sources, these values are unverified for decision use; the strict remedy is to blank/remove the published market figures.
2. **Validation scope is asymmetric.** `site/check.py` whitelists the allowed official domains on the primary site. `tools/check_site.py` only requires an HTTPS link on desk rows. Thus a link-presence PASS on the desk is not proof of official sourcing. README now states this accurately; strengthening that gate is a recommended follow-up.
3. **Evidence coverage is not full live verification.** `tools/verify.py` is strong internal arithmetic validation against the official standings snapshot but cannot prove the source page has not changed since collection, and it does not independently fetch each game result. The static checks likewise cannot refresh 345 injury rows. A scheduled, captured-page live audit with explicit retrieval timestamps and per-field diffs would reduce stale-data risk.
4. **The injuries/depth requirement is partly unanswerable with current sources.** The official injury report provides availability and status, but no confirmed 1st/2nd/3rd/4th-string rank. Do not infer replacement rank or unit impact from an injury list. The depth gap is flagged; a source-of-record for depth and snap/participation history is still needed.
5. **Counts drifted in historical notes.** Current checker output is recorded above. Historical board/README counts should not be repeated as today's observed totals without rerunning the tool.
6. **Coordination status inconsistency.** T15 remains marked `in progress` in the top task-board table while integration queue I8 and the PR log say it is done and merged. This audit logs that inconsistency; the original owner's row is not rewritten by this audit.

## Decision-quality interpretation

- **No luck-free claim is supportable from this sample alone.** The nine strategies are scored in walk-forward fashion, but the adjusted uncertainty includes zero and none is reward eligible. Keep any leaderboard as shadow-only until independent prospective records, calibration, multiple-testing control and a validated price source are available.
- **Correlation is not an odds model.** The 57-game historical outcome sample gives margin-versus-total Pearson `r = -0.3224`, with 95% interval `[-0.5432, -0.0598]`, under the committed desk calculation. This is an observational, small-window score correlation, not a probability of joint bet outcomes or a stable player-prop relationship. It must not be used to price parlays without prospectively validated joint probabilities and a source-backed odds/settlement definition.
- **Missing inputs that block responsible release:** official or licensed time-stamped odds and closing prices; player-game statistics for props; a verified depth chart or participation/snap data; consistent player identity and injury history; jurisdiction-specific reward/legal review; explicit settlement rules; and a prospective prediction ledger.

| 2 | Extend `tools/check_site.py` to validate source domains and source tier per value/cell, rather than mere HTTPS presence per row. | Makes the strict source rule enforceable on every page build. | Add a fixture with a T3-only value and prove the gate rejects it; permit exceptions only when explicitly classed and review-flagged. |
| 3 | Add a live-source snapshot/diff workflow for standings, schedules and injury reports. | Detects staleness and records what was actually checked. | Timestamped source capture, row/field-level diff, fail-closed behavior when a source is unreachable or its format changes. |
| 4 | Do not score player props, depth-impact signals, parlays or rewards until paired player stats, lineup hierarchy, prices and settlement rules have official/authorized provenance. | Keeps leaderboard rewards tied to reproducible skill evidence. | Separate gates per market; prospective shadow period and corrected confidence intervals before any prize. |
