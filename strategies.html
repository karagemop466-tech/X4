Leaderboard and rewards

Strategies are scored on out-of-sample accuracy, and a strategy only earns reward points if it clears every gate. The significance gate is what keeps a lucky streak out of the top of the table.

Controlling for luck

Which strategies are actually the same bet

Two strategies whose errors move together are not two pieces of evidence - they are one signal counted twice. The matrices below are computed from the same out-of-sample games. A value near 1.00 means the two strategies fail on exactly the same games, so ranking them against each other would overstate how much independent skill exists.

Calibration

Are the probabilities honest?

A forecast that is consistently too confident is worse than no forecast. For each offset the model states the probability that the true total exceeds its own total plus that offset; a well-calibrated model's stated probability matches how often it actually happened.

Tolerance 0.12
Stability

Does the edge hold week to week?

An improvement that appears only in a few weeks is not a reliable edge. Gate G5 requires the improvement against the league-constant benchmark to be positive in at least 60% of evaluated weeks.
