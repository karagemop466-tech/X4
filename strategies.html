Controlling for luck

Which strategies are actually the same bet

Two strategies whose errors move together are not two pieces of evidence - they are one signal counted twice. The matrices below are computed from the same out-of-sample games. A value near 1.00 means the two strategies fail on exactly the same games, so ranking them against each other would overstate how much independent skill exists.

Are the probabilities honest?

Risk control

Demotion gate

A live strategy that falls outside its own evidence is sent back to paper trading. The gate is a project design choice (no official source publishes one) and is published so it can be applied consistently.

Risk control

Demotion gate (live → paper)

Design choice, not an official rule

Rule: A live strategy is sent back to paper trading if, over any rolling window of 5 live games, its cumulative point-spread error exceeds the upper bound of its Bonferroni-adjusted bootstrap confidence interval, or its calibration gap exceeds 0.12 on those 5 games.

Action: Move the strategy from live to paper trading. It resumes live trading only after 10 consecutive paper-trading games within tolerance.

Basis: design choice by this project; no official source
