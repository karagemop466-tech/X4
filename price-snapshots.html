Originated market lines

We originate our own lines. Each market (spread and total for each Week 11 game) carries an opening fair line - our model's estimate (S3 strategy) derived from official NFL.com results - and a closing line that is recorded when the game closes. This build runs before kickoff, so every snapshot here is an opening snapshot.

Price-snapshot store for the markets we originate. The opening fair line is our model's estimate (S3 strategy) derived from official NFL.com results; it is not an official or sportsbook line (Flag F-23). The closing line is recorded when the game closes and is blank until then. Market lines themselves are blank (Flag F-18).
How to read this store. The fair line is our own originated price - a model estimate, not an official NFL betting line and not a sportsbook price (Flag F-23). The market line is blank because the NFL publishes no official betting lines (Flag F-18). The closing line is filled in after each game closes, so this store becomes a record of opening vs closing for every market we originate.
