Originated market lines

We originate our own lines. Each market (spread and total for each Week 11 game) carries an opening fair line - our model's estimate (S3 strategy) derived from official NFL.com results - and a closing line that is recorded when the game closes. This build runs before kickoff, so every snapshot here is an opening snapshot.

Two different quantities sit side by side here and must not be conflated. Our fair line is an originated model estimate (S3 strategy) derived from official NFL.com results - it is not a sportsbook price (Flag F-23). Market open / close are captured DraftKings prices read through the ESPN odds endpoint on 2026-11-14, linked in every row; the league publishes no betting lines, so these are labelled non-official (Flag F-18). Where no price was posted, the cell is blank and says which phase is missing. Fair − market is the difference, published so a disagreement between this model and the market is visible as a number and never mistaken for an edge.
How to read this store. Two different things are recorded here and they must not be confused. The fair line is our own originated price - a model estimate derived from official NFL.com results, not a sportsbook price (Flag F-23). The market line is a captured DraftKings price read through the ESPN odds endpoint on 2026-11-14, printed with its link, and is not an official NFL line because the league publishes none (Flag F-18). The closing column fills in once a game closes, so the store becomes a record of opening versus closing for every market, with the third column - the difference - published separately on Model lines.
