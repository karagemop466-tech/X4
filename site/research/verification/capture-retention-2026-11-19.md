# Capture retention and public/private data-layer audit — 2026-11-19

## Executive finding

The existing F-75 wording is inaccurate about the **current** capture tree: it says compressed NFL/club source pages are committed to the public repository. The current public `main` tree and the privacy-policy commit's tree contain only `data/captures/manifest.jsonl`; the current workflow ignores `.gz` captures and does not configure `X4_CAPTURE_RAW_DIR`. The manifest records hashes and metadata, but no raw capture is present in this checkout or evidenced as retained in a private store.

F-75 nevertheless remains **open**. The repository is public and contains normalized, player-level availability datasets; raw converted Game Book text is also tracked in the public repository, and the Pages artifact renders player-level rows. Thus the requested public-analysis/private-data boundary is not in place. No source-retention or redistribution terms were assessed here, and no legal conclusion is made.

## Decision and recommended safe path

- **Do not describe hash-only metadata as an immutable, replayable raw snapshot.** A hash proves equality only if the bytes are retained somewhere accessible for verification; this checkout has no such body for the manifest entries above.
- **Keep source bytes out of the public repository.** Before retaining them, record a terms/retention decision and configure a durable private store outside the repository. The workflow currently configures neither that store nor a private-storage location.
- **Until that boundary exists, do not call the current public row-level pages a private data layer.** The conservative target is to publish only aggregate/derived outputs and official-source links, while row-level evidence stays in an authorized private store. No current source terms or permission were inferred in this audit.
- **Keep availability-dependent backtests gated.** Week 10 rows are a dated historical snapshot, not Week 11 availability. The live Week 11 report has not been accepted by the committed schema gate or transformed into a verified dated dataset/delta here.

## Scope and unresolved items

This audit corrected the evidence statement only; it did not remove or rewrite any historical data, change the capture workflow, transcribe the Week 11 report, or make a source-terms determination. F-75 remains open until the private-store and public-output requirements have a verifiable implementation. The Week 11 report transition should be handled as a separate freshness/data-ingestion task; its current official source is linked above.
