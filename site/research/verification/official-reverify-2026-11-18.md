### Round-trip check (the check that makes the refresh safe)

The 13 new scores were transcribed from the schedule page; the 32-club standings were
transcribed separately from the standings page; the two were then required to agree
exactly, cell for cell. They do. A single mistyped score would break at least one
club's PF or PA, so the agreement is evidence about **both** reads at once, not a
spot check.
