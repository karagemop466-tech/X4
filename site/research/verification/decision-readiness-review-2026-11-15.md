## Questions that block a reasonable decision

1. **What does a reward recognize: forecast skill, tradable edge, or realized return?** Current evidence separates signal and staking, but no strategy passes its skill gates. **Blocker:** reward eligibility. **Clear when:** a frozen prospective strategy passes the published proper-score / uncertainty gates and the reward rule is fixed before evaluation.
2. **What price is being forecast against?** NFL.com publishes game facts, not a sportsbook quote. The archive cites third-party ESPN odds data for some market prices, while the integrity store has separate source/provenance semantics. **Blocker:** CLV, execution, ROI and market-consensus claims. **Clear when:** a licensed, timestamped operator/market feed is selected, its historical backfill and settlement policy are documented, and each price row has a direct source URL and retrieval time. NFL sources can verify game identity/result, not the betting price.
3. **Can the replacement's role be confirmed before kickoff and after the game?** Club depth charts do not establish game-day snaps; 31/32 chart coverage and zero measured rows remain. **Blocker:** injury/depth feature, replacement deficiency and player prop analysis. **Clear when:** official Game Books / official snap evidence and dated availability/inactive records join to every player-game, with conflicts retained rather than guessed.
4. **Can a parlay or prop combo be scored as a joint event?** No validated joint model/sample is present. **Blocker:** combo leaderboard. **Clear when:** joint probabilities beat a registered benchmark on chronological holdouts and the operator's push/void rules are captured alongside joint prices.
5. **Are published row-level values demonstrably sourced?** F-39 leaves 191 desk rows and 26 opening price points without source URLs; other source links may be third-party even where present. **Blocker:** strict source-verification claim. **Clear when:** each data-bearing row points to evidence that supports that value; model-derived values link to their official inputs and formula; unsupported items remain blank and flagged; the gate checks source host/tier and semantic role.
6. **Is the dataset genuinely point-in-time?** The repository has timestamped/hash-bearing snapshot records, but the Pages workflow does not schedule captures. The committed Week 10 injury snapshot also has later reported drift (F-34/F-40); this run did not re-check those live values. **Blocker:** leakage-resistant historical replay and availability ablations. **Clear when:** scheduled captures begin, preserve raw body/hash/time, record conflicts and parser/schema changes, and a season of snapshots can be replayed as known pre-kickoff.
7. **What constitutes an official starter and a replacement string?** Do not infer from roster ordering, jersey number, experience, salary, or a depth-chart column that names multiple co-equal players. **Blocker:** starter-out deficiency matrix. **Clear when:** Game Book starter evidence and snap share are stored separately from club depth-slot groups, including shared/empty slots and roster transactions.

## Recommended order of work

1. **Protect the trust boundary first:** make unsupported cells blank; classify evidence as official league, official club, official operator/provider, third-party, or internal calculation; keep F-39 visible. Do not relabel ESPN odds as NFL-official.
2. **Freeze shadow rules now:** version and pre-register each strategy, train/validation/final-holdout by season, chronological walk-forward grading, benchmark controls, game-cluster uncertainty, multiple-testing adjustment, and a demotion-to-paper rule. Exclude backtests from the prospective leaderboard.
3. **Build the point-in-time evidence spine:** scheduled immutable injury/depth/inactive/game-book/result captures with UTC timestamps, content hashes, parser versions, raw retention policy, freshness alarms, and explicit conflict records.
4. **Complete availability evidence before modeling it:** verify the missing club depth source route, represent depth as named groups, join injuries to official inactive/starter/snap records, then run identical with/without-availability chronological ablations. Never subtract a fixed injury penalty.
5. **Only then expand markets:** establish an authorised price policy and opening/current/closing snapshot convention; price singles first. Keep sides, totals and moneylines independently reported. Keep parlays and player-prop combinations blocked until joint evidence exists.
6. **Keep the Suggestions review queue decision-oriented:** for each proposal, show the evidence, missing datum, blocker, numeric exit threshold, expected effort, risk, and a two-sided “what would change our mind” test. Do not automatically implement owner/legal/data-policy choices.
