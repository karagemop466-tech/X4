THE STORE

Opening and closing numbers, kept apart and never overwritten

A single "line" column cannot answer the only question that matters — whether a number moved, and which way. Every market here therefore carries open_* and close_* as separate fields, with the UTC time the reading was taken and the URL it was read from. A re-read appends a new row; it does not edit the old one.

THE FIVE RULES

Why this store can be trusted

APPEND-ONLY
RULE 1

Open and close are separate columns

A market with one reading says so — close_kind reads same_as_open or current_as_close. Nobody can mistake a single quote for a movement.

RULE 2

Nothing is overwritten

A re-fetch appends. The file therefore grows into an audit trail rather than being silently rewritten.

RULE 3

Every row carries provenance

retrieved_utc and source_url are mandatory. A row without a URL fails the build.

RULE 4

A missing number stays blank

No interpolation, no carry-forward. Blank means the upstream did not publish it at that reading.

RULE 5

Third-party prices are labelled

Prices are a commercial product. Every row is tier T3 until a licensed feed is authorised, and the label travels with the number.

THE STORE

Every reading, with its source

The line is the spread or the total; the price is the odds paid on it. The moneyline carries a price and no line, because a moneyline has no spread attached.
