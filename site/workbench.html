HOW THIS WORKS

Score first, decide second

Every proposal is scored on the same four dimensions, 1 to 5, by one rubric printed below. The weighted score orders the list; it does not decide anything. Your Accept / Hold / Reject choice is stored in this browser and builds a shortlist you can act on. Nothing on this page is implemented by reading it.

THE RUBRIC

Four dimensions, one definition each

1–5 SCALE
CriterionWeightQuestion it asksHow to score it
decision_value0.4Does it change a decision someone has to make this season?1 = no decision depends on it, 5 = several blocked decisions depend on it
confidence0.3Is the underlying evidence verifiable in this repository today?1 = would need data we have never seen, 5 = the evidence is already committed
effort0.2How much work is it, in reverse (5 = cheapest)?1 = multi-week build, 5 = a copy change
risk0.1How little regulatory or reputational exposure does it add, in reverse (5 = safest)?1 = touches money or legal scope, 5 = internal only
Two dimensions are deliberately inverted. Effort and risk are scored so that higher is better (5 = cheapest, 5 = safest), which keeps every bar comparable. The labels say so on every card. A naive "effort" column where low is good would silently invert the weighting.
