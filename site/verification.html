AUDIT · 2026-11-18

What the checks above structurally cannot see

The ten checks re-derive claims from artifacts inside this repository. That has a blind spot the T46/T48 audits found: a stale artifact can agree with a stale artifact. On 2026-11-18 the game log and the standings snapshot were re-read against the live official pages and both were one week behind - 13 of 14 Week-10 games were missing from the log - while tools/verify.py still passed, because it reconciles the log against that same stale snapshot. The correction is applied (log now 150 games, verify.py 486 assertions PASS) and the consequences are published as post-hoc, not as results: flag F-91 records the self-consistent verification pair, flag F-92 records that S1's +9.49% post-hoc improvement has a borderline significance interval that flips sign with the row ordering of the identical game set, and the leaderboard's shadow-period gate G8 keeps every strategy ineligible until the pre-registered Week-11 forward window is played and graded.
