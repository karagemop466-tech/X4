02 · Opponent-adjusted power

Who is actually good

A team's raw points-per-game flatters a soft schedule. These figures subtract the average rate of the opponents each team has faced and add the league average back, so beating good teams and beating bad teams are not worth the same. These are model estimates, not official values - the raw official columns beside them are.

How this was verified. The game log was reconstructed and then checked, automatically, against the official standings: tools/verify.py rebuilds every team's record and point totals from the 137 games and compares them to the NFL's own figures. All 32 teams matched exactly on all six measures, which also proves every individual score. The check runs in 460 checks and is part of the build.
