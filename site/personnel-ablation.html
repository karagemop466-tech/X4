Personnel ablation · T48

What a missing player has actually cost — measured, not assumed

The brief asks for injury and personnel ablations instead of fixed penalties. This page publishes the one ablation the committed official data can support, with its n and a 95% interval, and it states plainly which ablation the data still cannot identify.

Official Week 10 injury report ↗ · Official Week 10 schedule ↗ · Method note ↗ · Source code ↗

The finding, in one paragraph

24 of 28 clubs carried an “Out” player. There is no clean control group.

On the official Week 10 report, 24 of the 28 clubs in action are ruled at least one player Out; 8 carry a Doubtful; 0 carry no game designation at all. A with/without personnel test therefore cannot be identified from a single week’s report at the club level — the treatment is nearly universal. The measurable contrast (A1/A2) is the small clear group against the rest, and both intervals span zero: this data cannot tell the effect apart from noise, which is exactly why a fixed points penalty for a missing player is not defensible and is not published anywhere on this site.

Input verification

All four “clear” clubs re-read from the live official page

The whole comparison rests on four club-games. Rather than trust the committed CSV, the official Week 10 report was re-read row by row on 2026-11-18 and each clear club was confirmed to carry zero Out designations: BUF, LV, NYG, PIT. Three designated controls (SEA, CAR, HOU) and CLE were re-read the same way and matched row for row, including every designation. The tool asserts this before publishing the list, so the page cannot claim a verification the data no longer supports.

What this licenses

Read the interval before the mean

Allowed

  • Quoting a measured base rate with its interval as an input to a future prior.
  • Sizing the maximum a personnel term could plausibly move a Week 11 line.
  • Setting the sample the next capture must reach before a feature is trusted.

Forbidden

  • Adding “−N points per Out player” to any prediction.
  • Calling a depth-slot effect “priced in” or “ignored” by the market.
  • Publishing a personnel-adjusted fair line from this evidence.
Reproduce

Every number is recomputable

python3 tools/personnel_ablation.py rebuilds the CSV and prints this table; python3 tools/personnel_ablation.py --check fails the publication gate if the committed CSV is stale. The two inputs are the official Week 10 injury report and the official Week 10 finals, both already proved elsewhere by tools/verify.py and the capture check.
