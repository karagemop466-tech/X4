METHOD LAB

What genuinely moves a number, and what only looks like it does

Three questions in one place: which fundamentals carry real signal, which working practices stop a backtest from lying, and which candidate signals are buildable from sources we already hold. Status labels are honest: in production means it is already moving a published number, blocked means the input does not exist yet.

FUNDAMENTALS

The differentials that carry signal

Ranked roughly by how much a model gains from them, not by how interesting they are. The ones needing play-by-play are listed for completeness and marked, so nobody mistakes an aspiration for an input.

BEST PRACTICE

Nine rules that keep a backtest honest

Each of these exists because omitting it produces a number that looks better than it is. They are written down so that a future session cannot quietly skip one.

9 RULES
PracticeCategoryWhyWhere it livesOfficial source
Write the hypothesis before the backtestPre-registrationA hypothesis stated after seeing the result is a description of the data, not a prediction. Every entry in the trial log is dated before its result is known.the trial logresults reference ↗
Score the number, not the bankrollSkill separationProfit is a slow, noisy statistic. Beating the closing number is faster and much harder to fake, so it carries the ranking weight.signal-vs-staking.htmlofficial results ↗
Hold out by time, never at randomNo leakageA random split lets a model see week 12 while predicting week 11. Walk-forward by week or season is the only split that matches how the number is actually used.gates G3official results ↗
Count every trial you ranMultiplicityTwenty strategies tested at p < 0.05 produce one false winner on average. The trial count is part of the result, not an appendix.gates G6nfl.com stats ↗
Show the interval, not just the estimateUncertaintyAn estimate without its interval invites the reader to trust the decimal places. The interval is where the honesty lives.every tableofficial standings ↗
Regress to the mean on purposeShrinkageA club four games into a season is mostly league average with a small signal on top. Shrinking by games played is a prior, not a fudge, and it is printed in the model card.origination.htmlofficial standings ↗
Separate paper from live with a numeric gateDemotionA strategy that could be demoted by judgement will not be. The gate has to fire on a number, and the number has to be computable before the game.signal-vs-staking.html#demoteofficial results ↗
Let a null result stay nullIntegrityThe most valuable output of this project so far is that nine strategies failed the significance gate. Publishing that is the product working, not the product failing.strategies.htmlofficial results ↗
Never fill a blank to make a table look completeProvenanceA blank with a reason is information. A guess is contamination that travels into every downstream number.flagged.htmlofficial injury report ↗
