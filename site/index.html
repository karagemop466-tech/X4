Executive summary

Official NFL data, verified line by line. Model lines labelled as estimates. Nothing filled in that could not be confirmed.

This desk is built on the NFL's own pages. Every number in every table traces to an official NFL.com URL that a reviewer can open. Scores were rebuilt from a reconstructed game log and then proved against the official standings: 137 games, and every team's wins, losses, ties, points for, points allowed and winning percentage match the NFL exactly. Where an official source does not exist - market lines, depth-chart strings, player-prop prices - the cell is left blank and the gap is listed on the Flagged for review page.

What the work actually shows: the model has signal over a league-average benchmark, but the improvement is not yet statistically separable from luck, so the leaderboard marks every strategy ineligible for reward. That is the validation gate working, not a gap in the analysis.

How to read this site

Four rules

Rule 1

Every row has a source link

Each table row links to the official page it came from - a specific NFL.com game page, player page or news article - not a home page. Click it and check the value yourself.

Rule 2

Blank means blank

Where a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, the cell stays empty and the item is flagged. Nothing is inferred, estimated in place of a fact, or filled from an aggregator.

Rule 3

Estimates are labelled

Model output is always marked as a model estimate and always shown with the range across all nine strategies, so you can see where the model is confident and where it is guessing.

Rule 4

Luck is removed, not wished away

Correlated strategies are grouped rather than counted as rivals, significance is tested with a Bonferroni-adjusted bootstrap, and until the evidence clears the bar no strategy is declared a winner.
