01 · ORIGINATING LINES

Line Lab

A candidate signal is a hypothesis until it clears every gate. No fitted probabilities or claimed edges are supplied here.

PROPOSED METHOD
Core principle

Estimate the full outcome distribution, not only a point estimate. Compare model probability with a de-vigged market probability, account for uncertainty and price, and pass only when the advantage survives time-aware, untouched validation. A forecast is not proof of profit.

Correlation is not a constant. A receiver's yards and receptions, QB passing yards and receiver yards, or opposing team outcomes can be dependent in ways that change with usage, game state, and player availability. Do not treat a static Pearson coefficient as a parlay pricing model.

05 · PROPOSALS FOR HUMAN SELECTION

Suggestions

Review and prioritize these proposals before implementation. No proposal is represented as already built.

REVIEW BEFORE BUILD
P0 · FOUNDATION

Set scope & data rights

Choose the product, jurisdictions, permitted data use, source licensing, update schedule, and owner before collecting or redistributing odds/player data.

Decision needed: authorized data provider and budget.
P0 · TRUST

Versioned, point-in-time data

Store source URL, retrieval time, event time, version, missingness and correction history for each record. Preserve pre-game snapshots to prevent look-ahead leakage.

Decision needed: retention and source SLA.
P1 · MODEL

Baseline before complexity

Start with transparent team and player baselines; add injury/depth adjustments only after role impact can be measured on historical, point-in-time data.

Gate: reproducible walk-forward evaluation.
P1 · COMBOS

Model joint outcomes

For parlays and prop combos, estimate dependence directly; compare joint probability to payout and single-leg alternatives. Avoid assuming independent legs.

Gate: out-of-sample joint calibration.
P1 · OPERATIONS

Availability change alerts

Poll or ingest licensed/official injury updates, record status changes and stale feed state, then flag affected predictions for rerun—without asserting a lineup before confirmation.

Decision needed: data access / latency.
P2 · COMPETITION

Shadow leaderboard first

Require locked predictions before games, publish sample size and uncertainty, and run a prospective paper-only season before attaching rewards or money.

Decision needed: entry and reward rules.
