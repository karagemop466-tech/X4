Executive summary

The objective is to produce calibrated probabilities and positive expected-value opportunities—not simply pick winners. No model can eliminate luck. A defensible process reduces avoidable bias through timestamped prices, strict out-of-sample testing, uncertainty estimates, and prospective paper trading.

What is established

Official NFL materials describe injury-report practice and game-status categories. They do not provide a complete, ready-to-use historical odds and prop dataset for this project.

What is not established

No verified sample, source licensing, odds archive, player usage history, depth-chart timeline, settlement mapping, or strategy results are present in the repository.

Recommended next move

Resolve data access and evaluation policy first. Build an immutable, timestamped source ledger; then run preregistered baselines and paper-trade before any reward-bearing competition.

Signal research plan

Use signals only when each input is known at the prediction timestamp. Compare every candidate against a no-skill baseline and a price-implied probability with vig removed. Any signal remains a hypothesis until tested on locked future data.

Suggested probability and correlation approach

Estimate probabilities rather than binary picks. For two legs A and B, estimate P(A ∩ B) directly or through a validated conditional model; report correlation alongside the joint probability and uncertainty. Pearson correlation is not enough for tail-dependent outcomes. Measure dependence by market, shared game, role, and season using out-of-sample data. For parlays, compare model joint probability with the offered payout’s break-even probability after accounting for the actual terms.

Use hierarchical/shrinkage estimates for noisy defense-vs-position splits; report sample count and interval. Avoid using post-kickoff information, final injury status when prediction predates it, or future odds in training features.

Luck is not removable. No finite win streak proves skill. Require sufficient independent evidence, report confidence intervals and season-to-season variation, and publish failed strategies as well as winners. Do not rank by raw win rate or reward strategies selected after repeatedly probing the test set.

Injury and lineup monitoring specification

Capture each official report as a timestamped snapshot, not an overwritten current state. Preserve team, player identifier, listed position, injury label if published, each practice participation designation, game status, report date/time, source URL, retrieval time, and corrections. The official injury report distinguishes practice participation and game status; do not equate limited practice with confirmed absence or infer a diagnosis.
