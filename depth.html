02 · The cascade the brief asks about

Starter, then 2nd, then 3rd string

The brief asks the system to know when a 2nd-, 3rd- or 4th-string player has to replace a starter. That is only meaningful when the starter is out and the man behind him is out too. The detector is re-run on every build against the official report.

0 cascades found
No cascade exists in the official Week 10 data. In no position row of any of the 31 clubs is both the listed starter and the next man ruled Out or Doubtful. The detector is in place and is re-run on every build, so the week a club loses its starter and his backup at the same spot, the row appears here automatically. Until then, any model that assumes a second- or third-string cascade is happening would be inventing it.

What this page does not claim. A published depth chart is not proof of who took the first snap.
