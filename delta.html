Two dated official captures of the same Week 10 report exist in this repository: 345 rows read on 2026-11-14 and 347 rows read on 2026-11-15. Their difference is published here cell by cell instead of a static snapshot being republished. A level says who was listed; a delta says what the league actually changed - and it is the delta that a line has to react to.

The weekly mechanism

The scheduled six-hourly capture workflow (tools/capture_official.py) is built to keep this diff current for every future week automatically. Its scheduled runs are currently failing at the fetch step with an unknown cause (flag F-43), so this page is a demonstrated mechanism over committed captures, not a live series. The design pre-registration stands: each capture is stored with its clock time and hash, and the published artifact is always the delta between dated reads - never a level without a date.
