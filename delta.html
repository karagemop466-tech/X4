The weekly mechanism

The scheduled six-hourly capture workflow (tools/capture_official.py) is built to keep this diff current for every future week automatically. Its scheduled runs are currently failing at the fetch step with an unknown cause (flags F-47, F-48 and F-56), so this page is a demonstrated mechanism over committed captures, not a live series. The design pre-registration stands: each capture is stored with its clock time and hash, and the published artifact is always the delta between dated reads - never a level without a date.
