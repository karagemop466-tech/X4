08 · Evidence before implementation

Suggestions & decision readiness

This is a reviewable roadmap, not a claim that every feature is complete. It separates work already present from the gaps that still block trustworthy decisions. The safe path below is recommended by default; no user reply is needed to continue research or keep strategies in paper.

Do not treat open gates as picks

Executive recommendation

Keep the product in research and paper mode. Use official NFL and club records for factual inputs; retain dated raw captures and hashes; leave unconfirmed cells blank; and label any third-party market prices as third-party rather than official. No strategy should receive a reward or be represented as profitable until a pre-registered prospective evaluation passes its proper-score, uncertainty, sample, calibration, and price-evidence gates.

This conservative default resolves the policy questions without asking the user to make an unsupported choice. A licensed market source, historical inactive evidence, player-level official game-book data, and sportsbook settlement terms are not established just because a useful page exists.

Current repo evidence: the market and strategy pages, verification notes, and flagged register document the current state; start with Market & price archive, Gates & demotion, Decision checklist, and Flagged for review.

Discovery queue

How to close the gaps

These are the concrete information-gathering tasks that make the later modeling decisions possible.

  1. Freeze definitions before new evaluation: version each strategy, target market, forecast timestamp, benchmark, metric, holdout, uncertainty method, and demotion rule in git before the evaluation window begins.
  2. Collect point-in-time evidence: schedule an immutable archive of the official injury report and club depth/transaction sources; capture game-day inactive evidence and game-book participation after games; record raw hashes, source URLs, fetch times, and disagreements.
  3. Verify coverage rather than assuming it: maintain a 32-club source matrix for chart format, datedness, shared/empty slots, source changes, and parser status. Do not derive order from jersey number, salary, or experience.
  4. Test whether availability adds signal: compare identical models with/without verified personnel data on chronological holdouts; publish each estimate with n and uncertainty; include no-feature and simple strength baselines.
  5. Keep outcome products separate: first singles; then game parlays only after direct joint validation; player-prop combos last, after player data and bookmaker settlement rules are confirmed.
  6. Review source and freshness flags weekly: distinguish official facts, official club statements, third-party observations, and model outputs. A source/schema change fails the affected build and appears on Flagged for review.

Default decision until gaps close: publish analysis and uncertainty only; keep staking simulated; do not claim a verified price edge or issue rewards; leave unsupported values blank.
