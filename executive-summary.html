WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND

Two-sided, in both directions

To accept an edge
Out-of-sample, benchmark-relative proper score better than the market's; n at or above the pre-registered minimum; a game-cluster lower bound above zero after a multiplicity adjustment for every trial run. Any one missing and the result stays ineligible.
To reject a model
A benchmark-relative score no better than the market on the same games, a negative lower bound, or a calibration gap that survives re-fitting on a later season. A model that fails is demoted to paper, not deleted, so the failure stays on the record.
To distrust this page
If any figure here disagrees with the dataset it cites, the page is wrong. The build recomputes each one; a reviewer can check any row by following its link.
