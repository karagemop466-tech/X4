WHAT IS STILL OPEN

The one number that decides the rest

This layer closes the machinery: the store, the ordering, the three-column discipline, the two-score split, the demotion gate, the n-on-every-correlation rule. It does not close the evidence. The sample is 12 settled markets against a floor of 100 per market type, so every gate that needs evidence fails, and the honest headline is that this system has produced one originated strategy that the gate correctly refuses to promote. That is the machinery working.
