The nine strategies

What produces a line

Each strategy is a different way of turning the same verified game log into a forecast. They are scored on the Strategy Lab page.

IDStrategyFamilyWhat it doesInput source
S0League constantBenchmarkLeague average home/away scoring measured before the game. Uses no team information; this is the benchmark every other strategy must beat.NFL.com results ↗
S1Simple mean, opponent-adjustedTeam formUnweighted mean points for/allowed per team, adjusted for the average quality of opponents already faced.NFL.com results ↗
S2Exponentially weightedTeam formDecay-weighted team scoring and allowing (lambda=0.65), opponent-adjusted, no shrinkage.NFL.com results ↗
S3EW + shrinkageRegularisedDecay-weighted scoring/allowing (lambda=0.65) shrunk toward the league mean with k=4, opponent-adjusted.NFL.com results ↗
S4Fast EW + heavy shrinkageRegularisedFaster decay (lambda=0.80) with heavier shrinkage (k=6): trades responsiveness for stability.NFL.com results ↗
S5Median + shrinkageRobustMedian scoring/allowing shrunk toward the league mean with k=4: robust to a single blowout loss.NFL.com results ↗
S6Slow EW + heavy shrinkageRegularisedLong memory (lambda=0.45) with heavy shrinkage (k=8): the most conservative team-form model.NFL.com results ↗
S7Blend S3 and S6EnsembleEqual blend of the S3 (fast, moderate) and S6 (slow, heavy) regularised models.NFL.com results ↗
S8Last 5 games windowTeam formUnweighted mean over the five most recent games only, opponent-adjusted.NFL.com results ↗
Every strategy produces a spread and a total for every game. Parameters are listed on the Methodology page.
