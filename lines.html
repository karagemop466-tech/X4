04 · Model lines

Week 11 projections

Every one of the 13 games on the official NFL.com Week 11 schedule, with a model spread and total from each of the nine strategies. The range across strategies is shown alongside the headline figure: where the range is wide, the model does not know.

Source: nfl.com/schedules/2026/by-week/week-11
There is no market column. The NFL publishes no official betting lines, and no licensed odds feed has been authorised for this repository, so the market-line cell is empty rather than filled from an aggregator. Every figure in this table is a model estimate derived from official NFL.com results - it is a research output, not a price and not a recommendation.
