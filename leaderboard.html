Leaderboard & rewards

Design proposal · 0 entrants
THE TOURNAMENT

Compete on evidence, not streaks

A season-long competition where strategies earn rank only by clearing gates and posting risk-adjusted, uncertainty-disclosed performance. Rewards follow rank — never luck.

DESIGN · NOT YET RUNNING
Design principle

Short-horizon leaderboards are lottery tickets. This one requires pre-registration, minimum samples, multiplicity-aware scoring and a shadow period, so a hot week cannot outrun a validated edge. Amounts for rewards are intentionally blank pending owner/legal decisions (F-05).
