WHAT THE MODEL DOES NOT SEE

The deliberate blind spots

The ratio model reads points scored and allowed, and nothing else. It does not read injuries, the depth of the replacement behind an injury, rest, travel, weather, or who is starting at quarterback. Each of those is a candidate signal on the method lab, and each has to clear the gates before it is allowed to move a published number.
