Executive summary

Official NFL facts are linked where verified. Model lines are labelled as estimates. Evidence gaps are visible.

This desk uses official NFL sources for facts that have been verified. Every rendered data row on the desk pages now carries an HTTPS source link — the 2026-11-18 checker run tests every row on all 26 desk pages and reports 0 rows without a link, which closes flag F-39 (fixed line by line, not by weakening the gate). Two honest limits remain: the checker verifies link presence, not that each link supports the exact value beside it, and rows produced by this project's own computation cite the committed artifact that produced them rather than an official page — treat those as artifacts, not NFL facts. Unsupported values remain blank and flagged on the Flagged for review page. Separately, the reconstructed game log was checked against official standings: 150 games, and every team's wins, losses, ties, points for, points allowed and winning percentage match. Market prices are non-official captures, not NFL facts.

What the work actually shows: the model has signal over a league-average benchmark, but the improvement is not yet statistically separable from luck, so the leaderboard marks every strategy ineligible for reward. That is the validation gate working, not a gap in the analysis.

How to read this site

Four rules

Rule 1

Source status is explicit

Verified NFL fact rows link to the specific official page they came from. Rows this project computed cite the committed artifact (the exact CSV or script on GitHub) that produced the value; the checker proves every row carries a link, and the flag register records what is still unverified. No source is invented to fill a gap.

Rule 2

Blank means blank

Where a value cannot be confirmed from an official source, the cell stays empty and the item is flagged. Nothing is inferred, estimated in place of a fact, or filled from an aggregator.

Rule 3

Estimates are labelled

Model output is always marked as a model estimate and always shown with the range across all nine strategies, so you can see where the model is confident and where it is guessing.

Rule 4

Luck is removed, not wished away

Correlated strategies are grouped rather than counted as rivals, significance is tested with a Bonferroni-adjusted bootstrap, and until the evidence clears the bar no strategy is declared a winner.

F-68The multiplicity correction divided by 8 while performing 9 testsIrregularityresolvedtools/build.py computed the Bonferroni threshold as alpha / (n_tests - 1), i.e. 0.05 / 8 = 0.00625, on the assumption that the benchmark S0 was excluded. It is not: the bootstrap loop runs over every key in STRATEGIES, S0 included, so nine intervals are computed and nine comparisons are made against one threshold. The gate's own published definition is "Bonferroni-adjusted p < 0.05 across all trials", and the review page's S-15 text already described the denominator as 0.05 / 8 strategies for nine strategies - that is, the site published a rule it did not apply. Corrected in T48 to alpha / n_tests = 0.00556.A multiplicity correction that is one test too lenient is exactly the failure the correction exists to prevent: it makes each of the nine results look more significant than the family of nine tests permits. The correction was material: at 0.00625 two strategies (S1 at p = 0.00630 was not one of them, but the threshold decided S2 and S5's status at the margin) sat closer to the line than they do at 0.00556. After the correction S1 (p = 0.00030) and S8 (p = 0.00206) still clear, so no published conclusion flips - but the rule now matches its own definition, and that is the point.Official source ↗ Evidence ↗

F-67Two of the eight walk-forward strategies clear the Bonferroni sign test, and the primary metric is undecidedMethodopentools/sign_test.py, added 2026-11-18, recomputes the paired sign test on the current corpus (n = 118 evaluated games) instead of quoting a superseded 105-game figure. Against the Bonferroni threshold of 0.05 / 9 = 0.00556 (corrected in T48: the gate had divided by 8 while performing 9 tests), BOTH clear, where the previously published figure named only S1. Choosing the primary metric now, after seeing which one passes and by how much, is the exact failure pre-registration exists to prevent.
