WHY THIS EXISTS

Lineups move lines. Know what moved.

Official injury reports tell you who may not play. Depth charts tell you who replaces them. This page does both: verified report rows today, and the exact pipeline for 2nd/3rd/4th-string deficiency scoring on offense and defense.

DEPTH RANKS · F-02
Honest limitation, stated up front

On 2026-11-14 every nfl.com depth-chart URL tested returned 404 (league-wide page, team page, printable endpoint). So depth string ranks on this site are blank and flagged — never inferred from rosters or box scores. The pipeline below is ready for the day a verified depth source is connected (see Q10 and R-03).

SPECIFICATION

The measured version of this specification now exists: Depth & deficiency transcribes all 31 machine-readable official club depth charts (column order = depth position) and joins them to the official Week 10 injury report, so starter-out deficiency by position group is published with a source link on every row (Houston publishes an image only - flagged).

Why case F matters

It is the rule that keeps this system honest: when depth order cannot be verified, the model does not quietly assume the backup is "close enough". The gap stays unmeasured, the line stays unadjusted, and the uncertainty is published. That is what "remove luck, keep skill" looks like in the data layer.
