Suggestions (Review Queue)

A dedicated place to review suggestions and choose the best path before implementing anything. Every row below is currently at proposed. Nothing here has been built.

The review workflow

Stage 1
Proposed
Written up with the problem, the proposal, the benefit, the risk and the decision required. Nothing may be implemented at this stage.
Stage 2
Under review
Someone is actively weighing it. Still not implementable.
Stage 3
Approved
A named decision has been made, including any parameter values. Only now may it be implemented.
Stage 4
Implemented / Rejected / Deferred
Closed out, with the outcome recorded so the same ground is not covered twice.
Hard rule. A suggestion at status Proposed must not be implemented. That is the whole point of this page: the brief asked for a place where the best path is chosen first. Two exceptions were made in this build, and they are disclosed here: this page, and the Flagged for Review page. Both are review machinery — they create no model, no signal and no stake.

The queue

Recommended review order

  1. S-01 and S-02 first. Operating mode and eligibility. Everything else is downstream of whether real money can move at all.
  2. S-08, S-10, S-11 next. Licensing, the price feed, and the bet-log schema. These are prerequisites for any strategy ever being scored.
  3. S-05, S-06, S-07 after that. Sizing, limits and the promotion gate. These bound the damage if a strategy is wrong.
  4. S-13 and S-15 once data exists. Correlation estimation and the depth pipeline.
  5. S-17 and S-18 last. Rewards and the multiplicity correction, once there is something worth ranking.

How to record a decision

Set the row's Review status using the filter dropdown, and record the decision in COORDINATION.md with the date, the decision, and any parameter values chosen. A decision recorded only in someone's memory will be re-litigated.
