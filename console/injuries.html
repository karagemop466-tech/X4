What the official designations actually mean

From the NFL Competition Committee's revisions, published on NFL.com:

  • Practice report: Did Not Participate; Limited Participation (less than 100% of a player's normal repetitions); Full Participation (100% of normal repetitions). The "Out" category was removed from the practice report to avoid confusion with the game-status report.
  • Game status report: Out = the player will not play; Doubtful = unlikely to participate; Questionable = uncertain whether the player will play. The "Probable" category was eliminated because roughly 95% of players listed as Probable did in fact play.
  • Not disclosed: precise diagnosis, severity, treatment or recovery timetable. The report gives a body part or condition only.

Source: NFL.com — Competition Committee approves revisions to injury report. Empty game-status cells on the official page are rendered here as unverified, not read as "will play" (F-07).

Gap analysis — why R-16 and R-17 are not met

Data-quality traps found in the official report

  • Non-injury entries inside the injury column. The official report carries entries such as "Not injury related - resting player" (Jaguars S Jessie Bates, Jaguars DT Jordan Davis) and a combined "Knee, Not injury related - resting player" (Rams LB Myles Garrett). A naive parser would count these as injuries and inflate an injury signal. Flagged as F-08.
  • Empty game-status cells. A large number of listed players have no game status at all on the official page. The meaning is not stated on the page, so it is left uninterpreted. Flagged as F-07.
  • Possible page staleness. An NFL.com roundup (Nov 10) reports the Broncos traded for Chargers LB Daiyan Henley, yet Henley still appears on the Chargers' Week 10 injury report. Flagged as F-10.
  • Do not flag rosters from memory. The 2026 league year differs materially from prior seasons — verified example: Aaron Donald retired in 2024 and then unretired to play for the Rams in 2026. Only internal inconsistencies and same-session source conflicts may be flagged. Flagged as F-09.

Relevant official roster rules

From operations.nfl.com:

  • The active/inactive ("53-man") list remains at 53 players for the regular season and playoffs. A club may expand to 54 or 55 only through the standard elevation addendum, using practice-squad players.
  • The gameday ("Active") list is 48 players if the club has at least eight offensive linemen (primary position C, G or OT) on it, and 47 if it has fewer than eight.
  • Under the standard elevation addendum an elevated player reverts to the practice squad on the first business day after the game.
  • Caution: the same official page states both that the addendum "can be used by a team for a maximum of two regular-season or postseason games per player per season" and that "each practice squad player can be elevated for a maximum of three regular season games in the same League Year". Those two sentences disagree. Flagged as F-16; no value is recorded here.
