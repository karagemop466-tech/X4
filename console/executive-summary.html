Executive Summary

The important details, in the order that matters. Every row carries a status and a source. Where we do not know something, the value is blank rather than estimated.

The one-paragraph version

The repository was empty — one commit containing only a README — so there was no existing site to review or improve; this console was built from scratch. What exists now is a complete research and governance scaffold: 35 requirements tracked, 32 open questions, 18 suggestions queued for review, 9 validation gates, 12 candidate strategies, and a full set of verified 2026 NFL data (all 32 clubs' standings, the Week 10 schedule, and 345 official injury-report rows). What does not exist is any evidence that any strategy works, because there is no historical odds or play-by-play data to test against, and because two of the brief's core requirements — knowing when a backup replaces a starter, and originating our own lines — depend on data for which no official primary source exists. The recommendation is to resolve the nine P0 questions before writing any more model code.

Key facts

What we verified, and where

  • Standings. All 32 clubs, 2026 regular season — nfl.com/standings. Reproduced in full on the NFL Data page.
  • Injuries. 345 player entries, Week 10 2026 — nfl.com/injuries. Reproduced in full on the Injuries page, with empty official cells left empty.
  • Schedule. 14 Week 10 games plus the official bye list — nfl.com/schedules.
  • Roster rules. 53-man active list, standard elevation addendum, practice-squad elevation limits — operations.nfl.com.
  • Injury-report definitions. Out / Doubtful / Questionable and DNP / Limited / Full — NFL Competition Committee revisions.
  • Gambling policy. Player and staff prohibitions — NFL.com and NFL corporate communications.

The three decisions that unblock everything

Decision 1
Paper or real money?
Sets legal exposure, tax, KYC and whether rewards are prize money. Recommend paper until the jurisdiction is confirmed. (Q-01, Q-02)
Decision 2
Which data do we license?
Without play-by-play and price history nothing can be validated. This is a budget decision, not an engineering one. (Q-09, Q-10, Q-11)
Decision 3
Who may promote a strategy?
Promotion to live stakes needs a named approver and an audit record, or an unvalidated strategy will eventually reach real money. (Q-25, S-07)

What we are deliberately not doing

  • Not publishing a correlation number we have not estimated. Sixteen pairs are enumerated on the Correlation page with an estimation method attached, and every value cell is blank. A wrong correlation makes a parlay price confidently wrong, which is worse than having no price.
  • Not scoring the leaderboard on profit. Profit separates from luck far too slowly. In the cited worked example, even after 1,000 bets there was roughly a 1-in-10 chance that luck alone produced a 2% profit where the expectation was a 2% loss. The leaderboard scores closing-line value instead.
  • Not filling in blank official cells. Where the NFL's injury page shows an empty game status, this site shows it as unverified rather than reading it as "will play".
  • Not guessing depth-chart URLs. Only the one club site verified this session has its URL published on the Injuries page. The other 31 are blank, because a guessed URL is a broken link.
  • Not implementing any suggestion before review. All 18 sit at Proposed.
