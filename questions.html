Questions, answers and gaps

A decision here is only reasonable once the 'what is still missing' column is either answered or explicitly accepted as a risk. Everything in the 'what we already know' column traces to the official source on the right.

10 questions
#QuestionWhat we already knowWhat is still missingDecision it blocksHow to close itSource
1What decision are we actually trying to make?The repository now holds a verified game log (137 completed 2026 games), the official NFL.com standings for all 32 teams, the official Week 10 injury report and the official Week 11 schedule with kickoff times, networks and bye teams.The market definition (point spread, total, player prop, or a proprietary rating), the decision horizon (single game, week, or season) and whether the output is informational analytics, a paper-trading competition, or a wagering product.Reward design, data budget, legal scope, and which of the strategies on the Strategy Lab page is even relevant.Write a one-page charter naming the market, the horizon, the decision owner and what 'success' means, then re-score every suggestion against it.Official reference ↗
2Where do betting lines come from, and do we price our own lines or trade them?The NFL publishes no official betting lines. Every spread and total on this site is a model estimate derived from official NFL.com results, computed by tools/build.py. NFL Game Operations publishes the rules of the game itself.A licensed, timestamped odds feed; which sportsbook and market format; the vig-removal convention; whether prices are compared against a market or the model sets its own price.Every expected-value, ROI, bankroll, Kelly and parlay-pricing calculation. Those cells are blank on this site and will stay blank until a feed is authorised.Either obtain a licensed odds feed with a documented history, or formally decide the project is market-independent and scores predictions only.Official reference ↗
3How will 'a 2nd- or 3rd-string player replaces a starter' be measured?The official NFL.com injury report gives practice participation and game status for every listed player on all 28 teams that played in Week 10. NFL.com news confirms specific starter changes (Seattle, Cleveland, Arizona, Miami) and long-term absences (Baltimore, the LA Rams, Green Bay).An official league-wide depth chart, snap counts, and a point-in-time record of who actually started each game. NFL clubs publish their own depth charts, but the league publishes no consolidated version.Quantifying what an absence is worth in points, ranking unit depth, and any player-prop model built on role.Ingest the official NFL.com game books (box scores) to build snaps and starters, and archive club depth charts with retrieval timestamps.Official reference ↗
4Which long-term absences are missing from the public injury report?The public Week 10 injury page does not list players on injured reserve, reserve/nontheatre injured list or physically unable to perform. Baltimore's starting quarterback, for example, appears only in league news, not in the report table.A complete, machine-readable IR/PUP/NIR list per team, per week.Multi-week absence tracking and any team-strength adjustment built from availability.Pull each club's own weekly official injury report (clubs publish them and the league links them) and archive them weekly.Official reference ↗
5How many games are enough to call a strategy skilful rather than lucky?105 out-of-sample games are available (Weeks 3-10). The best strategy improves point-spread MAE over the league-constant benchmark, but its Bonferroni-adjusted bootstrap interval still contains zero, so no strategy passes the significance gate.More seasons of official history, and a pre-registered minimum sample size for the reward contest.Awarding rewards, declaring a winner, or claiming an edge.Pre-register the sample size and the metric now, then keep scoring prospective predictions so the evidence accrues on a published timeline.Official reference ↗
6Is a 6-2 team actually good, or just fortunate?Official points for and against are verified for every team. A Pythagorean-style check, an opponent-adjusted margin and a home/road split are all computable from those official totals.Drive-level data: snaps, possessions, explosives plays, red-zone rate, turnovers and play-call mix. None of these is exposed by the free official pages in a single fetch.Separating a genuinely strong roster from one with a soft schedule and a few lucky wins.Ingest the official game books (NFL.com box scores) to build drive and possession features on the same point-in-time discipline used here.Official reference ↗
7What counts as a bet, and how is it settled?NFL Football Operations publishes the official rulebook that governs the games themselves. The published Week 11 schedule also carries the league's flexible-scheduling and future-week rules.Sportsbook-specific settlement: pushes and voids on postponed games, stat corrections on player props, and treatment of a cancelled game in a season.Backtest correctness and any realised-profit figure.Record the rulebook version and the market's settlement rules alongside every backtest, and keep a copy of both.Official reference ↗
8Who may take part, and in which jurisdictions?This is a public research repository published with GitHub Pages. It publishes no picks, accepts no bets and pays no prizes.Jurisdiction, age gating, and the prize structure if rewards ever become monetary or wagering-related.Any real-money reward mechanism or public competition.Obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review before attaching value to a leaderboard position.Official reference ↗
9How will the data stay fresh and point-in-time?Every official page used here is re-fetchable on a schedule, and the pipeline is scripted and reproducible: tools/verify.py proves the data, tools/build.py regenerates every published number.An automated fetcher with change detection and a dated snapshot archive, plus a nightly capture of the official injury report.Prospective evaluation, which is the only way the significance gate can eventually be passed.Add a scheduled job that stores a dated copy of the standings, the weekly injury report and the weekly schedule, then rebuild the site from the archive.Official reference ↗
10Which metric decides the leaderboard?The leaderboard currently scores seven gates: data integrity, out-of-sample discipline, minimum sample, statistical significance, weekly stability, calibration, and exclusion of the benchmark. Points are provisional until all seven pass.The reward scale, tie-breaks, disqualification rules, and the treatment of strategies that are statistically indistinguishable from each other.A fair competition and any published reward.Ratify the scoring formula and the correlation rule before the first prospective round.Official reference ↗
